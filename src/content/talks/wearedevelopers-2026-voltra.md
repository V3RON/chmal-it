What if you could ship native iOS Live Activities and Dynamic Island experiences without writing a single line of Swift? This talk tells the story of Voltra, a library born from a viral tweet and a collaboration between Saul Sharma and Callstack to bridge the gap between React and native iOS extensions.

We go behind the scenes of building a custom React renderer from scratch to translate JSX into SwiftUI primitives, bypassing the traditional "Swift wall." The talk covers the platform's extreme technical constraints, from the tiny 4 KB data limit to server-driven UI that does not require a pre-shipped implementation.
